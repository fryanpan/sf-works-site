    Dear Judge Massullo,

    In accordance with Penal Code 933 and 933.05, the following is in response to the 2024-2025
    Civil Grand Jury Report, “Uncontrolled Burn: Dimming the spark of Illegal Fireworks in San Francisco.” I
    would like to thank the members of the 2023-2024 Civil Grand Jury for their interest in the health,
    safety, and well-being of our residents and families.

    I agree that illegal fireworks pose serious public safety challenges for our communities. Beyond just
    the fact of the fireworks themselves, the events and gatherings that surround the fireworks can grow
    to include more insidious illicit activity, including sideshows and stunt driving, other explosives, and
    violence. Upon the release of your report and prior to the July 4, 2024 holiday, the named
    departments did coordinate to improve their messaging and response plan, with significant success,
    and I appreciate the Jury’s role in that improvement.

    Despite agreeing that we can and must do more to improve coordination in response to this
    challenge, I will not be implementing the Jury’s recommendations to form an additional working
    group with annual reporting. Our departments coordinate closely to take an evidence-based
    approach to fireworks response, and they operationalize that work within the limitations of their
    staffing levels. Additional process and reporting will increase the administrative burden of these
    public safety services, which may be counterproductive. I will, however, ensure that our agencies
    continually examine and improve our response in alignment with the best practices and updated
    data, in accordance with the Jury’s report.

    A detailed response from the Mayor’s Office, the Fire Department, Police Department, the
    Department of Emergency Management, the City Administrator, and the Controller’s Office
    is attached.

    We appreciate the opportunity to comment on the Civil Grand Jury report findings and
    recommendations. I have tasked the Department of Emergency Management with continuing to
    coordinate and strengthen our response to these dangerous events. We are committed to improving
    the City’s response to illegal fireworks.

    Sincerely,




    London N. Breed
    Mayor

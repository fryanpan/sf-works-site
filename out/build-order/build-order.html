The plan's best 20 miles bring 66% of the residents it brings within 15 minutes of daily destinations

The North Star plan adds 145 miles of all-ages bikeways. Built in the order that brings the most people within 15 minutes per mile, the first year at 20 miles a year brings 66% of the residents the plan brings within 15 minutes of a job centre, grocer, library or clinic, from 14% of its miles. Most residents are already that close to a grocer, library or clinic, so that count tops out early. For children it's 44% of those the plan brings within 15 minutes of school, and for jobs within 15 minutes of home, which keeps growing as the plan is built, it's 19% of the plan's gain.

The plan runs out part-way through its last year at every rate

A chunk is built in the first year its miles fit. After the last chunk nothing is left to build, so each line stops. After 78 miles no chunk brings anyone newly within 15 minutes, so the other 295 are built in order of joining the network, share on the High Injury Network and length. The years after that point are not ranked by need.

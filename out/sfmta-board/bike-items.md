## What this does not say

- **The match is on words, not meaning.** The term list is deliberately broad — a false positive costs one read and a miss costs the finding — so expect items here that turn out to be about something else.
- **An agenda is what was scheduled.** An item can be continued, severed or withdrawn, and only the minutes record that.
- **Engineering public hearings are not in here.** They are where much bikeway legislation is actually approved, and they are not on Granicus; they are noticed on sfmta.com. That is a gap, not an absence.

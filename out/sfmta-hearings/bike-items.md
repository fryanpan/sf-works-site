## What this does not say

- **The match is on words, not meaning.** The term list is broad on purpose — a false positive costs one read, a miss costs the finding.
- **An item is a line or two and this prints the head of it.** Follow the order for the full text; it is linked on every date.
- **A decision is not an installation.** "Approved by the City Traffic Engineer for implementation" is a legislative act with no date attached to the paint. Join it to the bikeway layer's `INSTALL_YR` before saying something was built.
- **Items forwarded to the SFMTA Board are not final here.** Their approval is in the board record, `out/sfmta-board/bike-items.md`.

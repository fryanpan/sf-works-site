## What this does not say

- **`Phase` is undated.** It is SFMTA's own word for where a project stands, with no completion date behind it, so a project reading "Completed" is not evidence that anything was built in a given year. Join it to the bikeway layer or the board record before saying so.
- **A project's geometry is its study area, not its build.** A corridor polygon covers blocks the project may or may not touch.
- **The page prose is prose.** It is fetched so a person can read what a project proposes; nothing here parses it, and SFMTA rewrites those pages without changing the layer.

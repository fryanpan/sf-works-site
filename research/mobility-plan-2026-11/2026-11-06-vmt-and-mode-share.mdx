# VMT and Mode Share in San Francisco

VMT means vehicle miles traveled, and mode share means the percent of trips made by each way of getting around. MTC is the Bay Area's planning agency, SFCTA is the City's transportation authority, and BATS and TDS are travel surveys. "Modeled" means a computer estimate, "measured" means someone counted or a study observed it, and anything else is my arithmetic.

## 1. VMT Per Resident by Where They Live

**The average San Franciscan accounts for about 7 miles of driving on a weekday, and neighborhoods run from 1.5 to 11, leaving out Treasure Island.** That's MTC's model: 7.03 vehicle miles per resident in 2023, leaving out airport trips, trips beyond the nine counties and trucks ([MTC layer](https://services3.arcgis.com/i2dkYWmb4wHvYPda/arcgis/rest/services/mtc_vmt_home_work_pba2050p/FeatureServer), [definition](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=03108de4de7b45d8ad66f742da1a0407)). [primary — queried 2026-11-06] SF-CHAMP, SFCTA's model and the one SF Planning uses, gives 8.06 with [2012 zone rates](https://data.sfgov.org/d/g4ye-s75w) on today's population. [primary — queried 2026-11-06] No one measured either figure. The neighborhood rollup is mine, weighting each model zone by Census population, 2020-24 ([script](../../tools/travel/vmt-by-neighborhood.py)).

- **332,000 residents (40%)** live in the 12 neighborhoods at 9 miles or more, and they hold 58% of residents' miles.
- **287,000 residents (35%)** live in the 13 neighborhoods under 5 miles, and they hold 17%.
- **Off the chart:** Treasure Island, 12.2, with 2,400 residents. One resident in ten lives in a zone under 2.3, and one in ten in a zone over 10.9.
- **The largest share** is Sunset/Parkside: 75,100 residents, 14% of residents' miles.

### Where Workers in High-Mileage Neighborhoods Go

**Where people work barely differs by neighborhood, so jobs don't explain the spread. Car use tracks it.** Visitacion Valley and the Tenderloin both send 43% of working residents to jobs outside SF, yet they sit at 10.9 and 1.5 modeled miles a day. Visitacion Valley has 1.72 vehicles per household against 0.29, and 67% commute by car against 11% ([LODES 2023](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes/LODES8/ca/od/), [ACS 2020-24](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25044,B08301&geo_ids=05000US06075)). [primary — queried 2026-11-06] This covers jobs only, and we have no public data on where non-work trips go.

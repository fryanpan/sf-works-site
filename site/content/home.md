# SF Works Prototype – Reports Built on Public Data

<p class="lede">Prototype reports on how San Francisco is working, each built from data the city already publishes and each saying which data it uses and when we read it. They are prototypes: the numbers are checked, the pages are not finished.</p>

## The reports

<h3><a href="/transportation">Mobility <span class="tag">prototype</span></a></h3>

Our goal for getting around San Francisco, and the dashboards and reports that track it.

<h3><a href="/housing">Homes <span class="tag">prototype</span></a></h3>

What share of the city's new housing was affordable, against the floor the Planning Code set, rebuilt from the data behind the Housing Balance report the city stopped publishing in 2026.

<h3><a href="/permits">Permits <span class="tag">prototype</span></a></h3>

How long a building permit's first plan review takes, and whether that time is spent waiting in a queue or under review.

<h3><a href="/grand-juries">Grand Juries <span class="tag">prototype</span></a></h3>

What each Civil Grand Jury report since 2021-22 asked the city to fix, how the city answered, and whether it was done.

Nothing here says the city broke a rule. These pages show what the data shows; they do not judge whether the city met its requirements.

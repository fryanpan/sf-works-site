# Survey Results on What People Want

These are the published survey results from four of San Francisco's Community-Based Transportation Plans. They come from MTC's [planning program for low-income communities]({{ mtcUrl }}), which the Visitacion Valley plan calls [Equity Priority Communities]({{ vvPlanUrl }}). So the results speak for those neighborhoods, not the whole city.

{{# grid }}

## Visitacion Valley and Portola, 2021 to 2022

Respondents named up to four streets or intersections and ticked what they wanted on each. The summary doesn't say what the percentages are a share of.

{{# visitacion-valley }}

## Lake Merced, 2021

A survey mailed to nearby addresses, and the same survey online, asked which fixes should be built. It offered only five fixes, so in the grid above its ranks stop at 5.

{{# lake-merced }}

## Bayview, 2018

Respondents put 10 themes from past plans in order. The plan reports each theme's average rank, so a lower number is a higher priority.

{{# bayview }}

## Western Addition, 2015

Residents said what needs improvement, and the plan counted how often each topic came up. These are counts of mentions, not shares.
